\chapter{The tests}\label{chap:tests}

\section*{At a glance}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Goal} Show on concrete programs that hypotheses of the final theorem hold: the shipping fragment test accepts the program, and the entry point of
  \code{\#erase} returns the expected $\lambda_\Box$ program.
\item \lead{Main results} \code{hcollect} and \code{hrun} on NV-1 (a \code{casesOn} on
  \code{inductive B | f | t}), NV-P and NV-K (an \code{Eq.casesOn} on a constructor and on a
  lemma); \code{henv}, \code{hwf}, \code{he} and \code{hty} on NV-1; determinism of
  $\lambda_\Box$ evaluation.
\item \lead{Status} \stProved{} every node; the runs by kernel evaluation of the shipping code. The
  instances of the other hypotheses are open (Section~\ref{sec:open-registers}).
\item \lead{Nodes} \bpnodes{chap:tests}
\item \lead{Lean files} \leanfiles{proof/EraseProof/Test/}{NV1Data, NV1Model, NV1Run, NVPData,
  NVPRun, Run, LBEval}.
\item \lead{Reference} the hypotheses of MetaRocq's \code{erase\_correct}
  (\code{erasure/theories/ErasureFunctionProperties.v}).
\end{itemize}

\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Caveat} The tests discharge \code{hfrag} and \code{hrun} on the three programs, and
  the typing hypotheses on NV-1 only; \code{hview} and \code{hev} are open.
\item \lead{Caveat} The views of the tests are the closures as lists; \code{\#erase} reads the
  whole environment, which agrees with them on their names.
\end{itemize}
