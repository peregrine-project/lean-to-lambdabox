\chapter{The tests}\label{chap:tests}

\section*{At a glance}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Goal} Show on concrete programs that the hypotheses \code{hfrag} and \code{hrun} of the
  final theorem hold: the shipping fragment test accepts the program, and the entry point of
  \code{\#erase} returns the expected $\lambda_\Box$ program.
\item \lead{Main results} \code{hcollect} and \code{hrun} on NV-1 (a \code{casesOn} on
  \code{inductive B | f | t}), NV-P and NV-K (an \code{Eq.casesOn} on a constructor and on a
  lemma).
\item \lead{Status} \stProved{} every node, by kernel evaluation of the shipping code; the
  instances of the other hypotheses are open (Section~\ref{sec:open-registers}).
\item \lead{Nodes} \bpnodes{chap:tests}
\item \lead{Lean files} \leanfiles{proof/EraseProof/Test/}{NV1Data, NV1Run, NVPData, NVPRun, Run}.
\item \lead{Reference} the hypotheses of MetaRocq's \code{erase\_correct}
  (\code{erasure/theories/ErasureFunctionProperties.v}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Running the pure path in the kernel}\label{sec:tests-run}

The traversal state holds \code{Std.HashMap}s keyed by \code{Name}, whose hashing the kernel does
not evaluate; a run is evaluated one traversal call at a time, with the map accesses rewritten.

  \label{lem:EraseProof-Test-bind_ok}
  \lean{EraseProof.Test.bind_ok, EraseProof.Test.runPure_bind, EraseProof.Test.runPure_withReader,

  \srcloc{proof/EraseProof/Test/Run.lean}{47}

  action}, and turn the \code{Subarray} loops, which the kernel does not reduce, into lists.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{bind\_ok}: a bind whose first action returns \code{a} runs its continuation on
        \code{a};
  \item \code{runPure\_bind}, \code{runPure\_withReader}: \code{runPure} through a bind and a
        change of context;
  \item \code{withApp\_const}, \code{withApp\_app1}: \code{Expr.withApp} on a constant and on a

\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Caveat} The tests discharge \code{hfrag} and \code{hrun} only; the instances of the
  typing and evaluation hypotheses are open.
\item \lead{Caveat} The views of the tests are the closures as lists; \code{\#erase} reads the
  whole environment, which agrees with them on their names.
\end{itemize}
