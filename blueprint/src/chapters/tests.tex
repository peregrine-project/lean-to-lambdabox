\chapter{The tests}\label{chap:tests}

\section*{At a glance}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Goal} Show on concrete programs that \hl{every hypothesis of the final theorem holds},
  so that it is not vacuous, and apply it to them.
\item \lead{Main results} \code{erase\_correct} applied with all eight hypotheses to four
  programs: NV-1 (a \code{casesOn} on \code{inductive B | f | t}), NV-P and NV-K (an
  \code{Eq.casesOn} on a constructor and on a lemma), NV-2 (a \code{casesOn} that binds a data
  field and a proof field); the first-order corollary on NV-1 and NV-2; the lower bound
  \code{collectDeps\_ok\_of} on NV-1; determinism of $\lambda_\Box$ evaluation.
\item \lead{Status} \stProved{} every node; the runs by kernel evaluation of the shipping code.
  C12 checks the footprint of each \code{final} and \code{firstorder} instance.
\item \lead{Nodes} \bpnodes{chap:tests}
\item \lead{Lean files} \leanfiles{proof/EraseProof/Test/}{NV1Data, NV1Model, NV1Run, NV1Final,
  NV1Collect, NVPData, NVPModel, NVKModel, NVPRun, NVPFinal, NV2Data, NV2Model, NV2Run, NV2Final,
  Run, LBEval}; \leanfile{proof/tools/NVEmit.lean}.
\item \lead{Reference} the hypotheses of MetaRocq's \code{erase\_correct}
  (\code{erasure/theories/ErasureFunctionProperties.v}).
\end{itemize}

\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Caveat} No instance has a projection, a Peano literal, a quotient, a recursive type
  or an opaque; that their premises are satisfiable is argued, not shown.
\item \lead{Caveat} \leanfile{proof/tools/NVEmit.lean} checks the declarations of NV-1, NV-2 and
  NV-P against the kernel's; NV-K's lemma \code{hEq} is written by hand.
\item \lead{Caveat} The views of the tests are the closures as lists; \code{\#erase} reads the
  whole environment, which agrees with them on their names. The kernel checks the instances on
  the logical definitions; NVEmit runs the compiled code.
\end{itemize}
