\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Caveat} No instance has an indexed or mutual recursor, an opaque or a mutual
  structural block; that their premises are satisfiable is argued, not shown.
\item \lead{Caveat} \leanfile{proof/tools/NVEmit.lean} checks the declarations of NV-1, NV-2, NV-P,
  NV-Q, NV-R, NV-S, NV-L and NV-QP against the kernel's; NV-K's lemma \code{hEq} is written by hand.
\item \lead{Caveat} NV-S's view holds the compiled companion \code{add.\_unsafe\_rec} beside the
  closure, written out and compared by \code{NVEmit} with the elaborated environment's; the
  fragment test accepts the program through \code{isStructuralRecursive} under
  \code{structural := .kernel}, and rejects it under the default configuration.
\item \lead{Caveat} The views of the tests are the closures as lists; \code{\#erase} reads the
  whole environment, which agrees with them on their names. The kernel checks the instances on
  the logical definitions; NVEmit runs the compiled code.
\end{itemize}
