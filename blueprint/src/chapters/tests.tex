\chapter{Test programs}\label{chap:tests}

\section*{At a glance}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Goal} Discharge every hypothesis of \code{erase\_correct\_statement} on three concrete programs, so that the statement is not vacuous.
\item \lead{Main results} NV-0 (a polymorphic identity), NV-1 (\code{B.neg} on a two-constructor type), NV-2 (a \code{casesOn} with $\eta$-expanded constructors and a second type). Section~\ref{sec:tests-table} tabulates what discharges each hypothesis.
\item \lead{Status} \stProved{} every hypothesis on all three programs, and the shape check \code{declOf?} on blocks outside their own \code{all}, unsafe and nested; \hl{the conclusion is not instantiated}, since \code{erase\_correct} is not proved.
\item \lead{Nodes} \bpnodes{chap:tests}
\item \lead{Lean files} \leanfiles{proof/EraseProof/Test/}{NV0Data, NV0Premises, NV1Data, NV1Run, NV1Premises, NV2Data, NV2Premises, Run, DeclOf}.
\end{itemize}

\section{The programs and their hypotheses}\label{sec:tests-table}

Each program has a data node (its declarations as Lean generates them, checked against the elaborator by \code{NVEmit}) and a node for its hypotheses.

\begin{tabular}{p{0.2\linewidth}p{0.7\linewidth}}
\toprule
Hypothesis & Discharged by \\
\midrule
\code{hacc} & NV-0: the kernel. NV-1, NV-2: the kernel, except the one equation that con-leche's fold returns the stated environment, a premise of the theorem and a compiled \code{\#guard} that fails the build if false (DV-40) \\
\code{hty}, \code{hcert} & the kernel, by evaluating con-leche's inference on the term \\
\code{hclean}, \code{hview}, \code{hnopre}, \code{hfrag} & the kernel, by \code{decide} and symbolic evaluation of the fragment test \\
\code{hrun} & the kernel, by symbolic evaluation of the entry point (\code{NVEmit} runs the compiled code and compares the emitted program byte for byte) \\
\code{hmark} & the kernel, by symbolic evaluation of the oracle; its marks are the $\Box$s of \code{hrun}'s output \\
\code{hev} & an explicit derivation of \code{SrcEval}, with \code{CLOk} certificates by the kernel \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item The conclusion of \code{erase\_correct\_statement} is not discharged: that needs the theorem.
\item For NV-1 and NV-2, \code{hacc} rests on compiled evaluation of con-leche's fold (DV-40).
\item The programs have no recursion, no pre-definition and no Nat literal.
\end{itemize}
