\chapter{Tests and non-vacuity}\label{chap:tests}

\section*{At a glance}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Goal} Show that the hypotheses of the main statements can be discharged on concrete
  programs, relate the restated relations to lean4lean's, and pin the design choices of the
  divergence register by tests.
\item \lead{Main results} The model-side hypotheses of NV-1; \code{TrS} and \code{ProgEnv} are
  instances of lean4lean's \code{TrExprS} and \code{TrEnv'}; the register tests of DV-11 and
  DV-18; two instances of \code{Pure.isErasable\_sound}.
\item \lead{Status} \stProved{} every node; \stOpen{} the instances of the main statements other
  than NV-1's model side (Section~\ref{sec:tests-instances}).
\item \lead{Lean files} \code{proof/EraseProof/Test/\{NV1, Bridge, LBEval, Atoms, Oracle\}.lean};
  namespace \code{EraseProof.Test}.
\end{itemize}

\code{Erasure.oracleFuel} on a formal environment and is checked by \code{decide}.
