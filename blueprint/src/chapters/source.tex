\section{Marks}\label{sec:source-marks}

A mark is metadata with the anonymous key on a subterm, written by \code{MarkOf} one rule per case of the traversal.

\begin{definition}[Marked terms]
  \label{def:EraseProof-MarkOf}
  \lean{EraseProof.MarkOf, EraseProof.EtaLams, EraseProof.markMD, EraseProof.markErase, EraseProof.markEta, EraseProof.markReduced, EraseProof.bvarOccurs, EraseProof.etaContract, EraseProof.unmark, EraseProof.absFV, EraseProof.Fresh, EraseProof.oracle, EraseProof.forallView?, EraseProof.markArity?, EraseProof.MarkEta, EraseProof.MarkSat, EraseProof.MarkAlt}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:EraseProof-DeclsOf}
  \srcloc{proof/EraseProof/Spec.lean}{764}
  \lead{In short} \hl{\code{MarkOf decls cfg ls e em}: \code{em} is \code{e} with the traversal's decisions written on it}; \code{unmark} inverts it.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{markErase}, \code{markEta}, \code{markReduced}, \code{markMD}: a subterm erased, a $\lambda$ introduced by $\eta$-expansion, a reduced discriminant;
  \item \code{MarkOf}, \code{MarkEta}, \code{MarkSat}, \code{MarkAlt}: the marking relation, for $\eta$-expansion, saturated spines and alternatives;
  \item \code{oracle}, \code{forallView?}, \code{markArity?}, \code{Fresh}, \code{absFV}: the oracle's answer, the $\Pi$ it reads, the arity, a fresh local, abstraction;
  \item \code{unmark}, \code{etaContract}, \code{EtaLams}, \code{bvarOccurs}: remove the marks, contract an $\eta$, every $\eta$ mark sits on a $\lambda$.
  \end{itemize}
  \lead{Reference} none; DV-23, DV-32.
\end{definition}

  \label{lem:EraseProof-unmark-instantiate1}

  \leanok

  \srcloc{proof/EraseProof/Source/Marks.lean}{415}
  \lead{In short} \hl{\code{unmark} commutes with lifting, instantiation and universe instantiation}, instantiation on terms whose $\eta$ marks sit on $\lambda$s.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{unmark\_instantiate1'}, \code{unmark\_instLevels}, \code{unmark\_liftLooseBVars'}: the three commutations;
  \item \code{Marks.etaContract\_*}, \code{Marks.etaShape\_*}, \code{Marks.bvarOccurs\_*}, \code{Marks.lower\_*}, \code{Marks.inst\_lift\_cancel}: the de Bruijn facts they need.
  \end{itemize}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}

\section{Restriction to the closure}\label{sec:source-restrict}

The evaluation over the program is the evaluation over the closure the traversal erased.

\begin{lemma}[Evaluation restricts to the closure]
  \label{lem:EraseProof-SrcEval-restrict}
  \lean{EraseProof.SrcEval.restrict}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:EraseProof-SrcEval, def:EraseProof-ViewAgrees, lem:EraseProof-Restrict-aux}
  \srcloc{proof/EraseProof/Source/Restrict.lean}{708}
  \lead{In short} \hl{A source evaluation over the program is one over the closure}, and its value mentions only constants of the closure.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{hsub}, \code{hcl}, \code{hctors}: the closure is a closed sub-environment that keeps the constructors' inductives;
  \item \code{hmark}: markings over the program are markings over the closure;
  \item \code{hc}: the constants of the term, without $\lambda$ domains, lie in the closure.
  \end{itemize}
  \lead{Reference} \code{ML-V Source/Restrict}; DV-35.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{lem:EraseProof-Restrict-aux}
  \leanok
  \lead{Idea} Induction on the evaluation; each rule reads the same declarations over the closure.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Agreement over the closure]
  \label{lem:EraseProof-Restrict-aux}
  \lean{EraseProof.DepClosed, EraseProof.BodyConstsIn, EraseProof.Restrict.ConstsIn_getAppFn, EraseProof.Restrict.ConstsIn_peelLams, EraseProof.Restrict.ConstsIn_spine, EraseProof.Restrict.BodyConstsIn.of_constsIn, EraseProof.Restrict.BodyConstsIn_foldl, EraseProof.Restrict.BodyConstsIn_mkAppN, EraseProof.Restrict.BodyConstsIn_getAppFn, EraseProof.Restrict.BodyConstsIn_lift, EraseProof.Restrict.BodyConstsIn_instantiate1', EraseProof.Restrict.BodyConstsIn_instLevels, EraseProof.Restrict.BodyConstsIn_absFV, EraseProof.Restrict.findDecl_eq_some, EraseProof.Restrict.findDecl_sub, EraseProof.Restrict.findDecl_agree, EraseProof.Restrict.ctorAlts_congr, EraseProof.Restrict.blockCtorCount_congr, EraseProof.Restrict.casesInfo?_congr, EraseProof.Restrict.isConstAt_const, EraseProof.Restrict.casesInfo?_reads, EraseProof.Restrict.casesOnOf?_agree, EraseProof.Restrict.specialArity_agree, EraseProof.Restrict.unfold?_agree, EraseProof.Restrict.EvidentProp_agree, EraseProof.Restrict.isAtom_agree, EraseProof.Restrict.head_mem, EraseProof.Restrict.shortSpine_iff, EraseProof.Restrict.propInd_agree, EraseProof.Restrict.CtorValue.restrict, EraseProof.Restrict.natLitCtor_cases, EraseProof.Restrict.natLitCtor_restrict, EraseProof.Restrict.casesOnOf?_ind, EraseProof.Restrict.ctor_mem_of_casesOn?, EraseProof.Restrict.unfold?_read, EraseProof.Restrict.mapM_restrict, EraseProof.Restrict.mem_mapM, EraseProof.Restrict.recBlock?_restrict, EraseProof.Restrict.BlocksCong_sub, EraseProof.Restrict.forall_mem_of_rel, EraseProof.Restrict.MarkOf.bodyConstsIn}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:EraseProof-SrcEval}
  \srcloc{proof/EraseProof/Source/Restrict.lean}{19}
  \lead{In short} Every recognizer and reader of the semantics gives the same answer over the program and over a closed sub-environment.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{DepClosed}, \code{BodyConstsIn}: the closure is closed under dependencies; constants of a term without domains;
  \item \code{Restrict.*\_agree}, \code{Restrict.*\_congr}, \code{Restrict.findDecl\_*}: recognizers and lookups agree;
  \item \code{Restrict.BodyConstsIn\_*}, \code{Restrict.ConstsIn\_*}: the constant conditions are stable under spines and substitution;
  \item \code{Restrict.MarkOf.bodyConstsIn}, \code{Restrict.CtorValue.restrict}, \code{Restrict.recBlock?\_restrict}: marks, values and blocks restrict.
  \end{itemize}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  \lead{Idea} Case analysis on the reader, with membership of the closure for every name it reads.
\end{proof}

\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item Marks are the eraser's decisions; the oracle behind them is not verified (Chapter~\ref{chap:open}).
\item \code{SrcEval} has no rule for $\delta$ on theorems, opaques or \code{@[extern]} atoms under \code{.preferAxiom}.
\item The restriction ignores $\lambda$ domains: no rule reads them.
\end{itemize}
