\section*{At a glance}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Goal} Verify, in Lean 4, the shipping \code{\#erase} eraser of this branch ---
\code{Erasure.erase} and its term walk \code{Erasure.visitExpr} --- \hl{not an idealised model of it}.
\item \lead{Architecture} Follows Sozeau et al.'s certified erasure for Rocq ([JACM, Sec. 7.3--7.4],
after Letouzey [L]): a target calculus with weak call-by-value semantics; an erasability
specification and non-deterministic erasure relation refined by the implemented function; a forward
simulation; a collapse of non-determinism at first-order observations --- re-formalised for Lean's
term language and, via \code{lean4lean}, its kernel theory.
\item \lead{Main results} Theorem~\ref{thm:shipping_erase_correct_firstorder}
(\code{shipping\_erase\_correct\_firstorder}, Chapter~\ref{chap:capstone}): first-order source evaluation
is matched by the emitted program's evaluation.
\item \lead{Status} \stProved{} in Lean, no local \code{sorry}, but \textbf{conditional} on fourteen
binders, and \stOpen{}: the hypothesis set of every rung is jointly unsatisfiable, so every rung and
the capstone's observable conjunct are vacuous (Section~\ref{sec:intro:theorem}); \stAssumed{}
\code{hbridge}; \stInherited{} throughout, via \code{lean4lean}.
\item \lead{Lean files} \code{Erasure.lean}, \code{Capstone.lean}, \code{ErasureSpec.lean},
\code{Upstream.lean}, \code{Green.lean}, \code{test/Vacuity.lean}.
\item \lead{Paper} [JACM, Sec. 7.3--7.4]; [L]; the eraser's own design document is [R]
(Section~\ref{sec:intro:references}).
\end{itemize}

\section{The problem and the object of verification}
\label{sec:intro:problem}

\lead{In short} Peregrine's Lean frontend erases \code{Lean.Expr} to \lbox{} through one entry point,
\code{Erasure.erase}; this blueprint verifies that shipping function, not a model of it.

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Pipeline} Peregrine compiles proof-assistant programs through one Rocq-verified
middle-end whose input is \lbox{}; a frontend's only job is to produce that program. This repository
is the Lean frontend.
\item \lead{Entry point} \code{\#erase}, elaborated by \code{Erasure.eraseElab}, takes a term plus
an optional configuration, output file and \code{.mli} sidecar; it elaborates, calls
\code{Erasure.erase} and serialises the result.
\item \code{Erasure.erase} runs \code{Erasure.visitExpr} on the output of
\code{Erasure.prepare\_erasure}, from the empty state, returning the untyped program (declarations
plus main term) and inlining hints. The main theorem's two run hypotheses are exactly this split;
\code{erase\_run\_ok} (Chapter~\ref{chap:coldstart}) performs it formally.
\item \lead{Preparation} \code{Erasure.prepare\_erasure} rewrites the term with three passes ---
redirect constants to their \code{\_unsafe\_rec} companions (\code{replaceUnsafeRecNames}) and
Lean's LCNF passes \code{macroInline} and \code{inlineMatchers} --- and, only when \code{csimp} is
on, with \code{@[csimp]} replacements. The syntactic conjuncts speak of the \emph{prepared} term,
the observable conjunct of the term the user wrote; \code{prepare\_sound} connects the two. The
eraser also reads a compiler-facing body (the \code{\_unsafe\_rec} companion, top-level recursive),
not the kernel-facing recursor-based one; both are named hypotheses, not silent assumptions.
\end{itemize}

\section{The main theorem in one paragraph}
\label{sec:intro:theorem}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Given} the shipping eraser runs on $e$ from the empty erasure state under the pinned
configuration and returns $(\Gamma, t)$; the prepared term $pe$ it actually walks lies in the
supported fragment and translates into \code{lean4lean}'s model of the environment; the emitted
program satisfies peregrine's first-pass check \code{LBWfPeregrine};
\item \lead{Then} there are $\Gamma_{\mathrm{spec}}$ and an intermediate \lbox{} term $t_0$, with $pe$
erasing to $t_0$ (\Erases{}), $\Gamma_{\mathrm{spec}}$ erasing the source environment along everything
$t_0$ reaches (\ErasesEnv{}, each tabled body read at that declaration's own level scope), and
\Lower{} taking $t_0$ to $t$ and $\Gamma_{\mathrm{spec}}$ to $\Gamma$;
\item \lead{The observable} for every closed argument spine whose \lbox{} images carry erasures of
their own: if $e$ applied to it evaluates to a value $v$ whose type is a spine of an inductive
\emph{first order} in the model, then $v$ has a unique erasure, that erasure's lowering is box-free,
and the emitted program's evaluation of $t$ at the lowered arguments equals it exactly, under
\lbox{}'s weak call-by-value semantics.
\end{itemize}

\code{shipping\_erase\_correct\_firstorder} (\code{Capstone.lean}) is stated in full as
Theorem~\ref{thm:shipping_erase_correct_firstorder} in Chapter~\ref{chap:capstone}.

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Vacuous} \stOpen{} \hl{No environment satisfies the hypotheses of any rung.} Every rung
binds \code{A : UpstreamAsks env} together with \code{hfo : FirstOrderInd env Nat} and with
\code{P} and \code{htbl}, whose table declares \code{Nat}'s block; \code{green\_G5} and
\code{green\_G8} also bind \code{SpikeNatFacts}. Each of these is refutable together with \code{A}
at every environment (\code{doc/rework/07-STATUS.md}, section 1).
\item \lead{Why} \code{UpstreamAsks.constOriginExcludes} reads \code{ConstOrigin} off any
well-formed declaration list below \code{env}, and a block's constants are declarable there as
axioms; so the field is false at every environment declaring an inductive
(\code{test/Vacuity.lean}, \code{not\_upstreamAsks\_natEnv}).
\item \lead{The capstone} Its observable conjunct is guarded by \code{FirstOrderInd env I}, so under
its own \code{A} it applies only at an environment declaring no inductive at all.
\item \lead{Uniqueness is false} \code{Nat.zero} has two distinct erasures at the fixture
environment, by \code{Erases.ctor} and by \code{Erases.const} (\code{erases\_natZ\_two\_ways}); the
rungs' uniqueness conjunct is out of reach only because \code{UpstreamAsks} is false.
\item \lead{The repair} Read \code{ConstOrigin}, \code{CtorOf}, \code{IndInfo},
\code{CasesOnShape} and \code{IndBlockBelow} off \code{env}'s own declaration list, as MetaRocq's
\code{lookup\_env} does. Exclusion and uniqueness then follow from \code{VEnv.WF'.consts\_origin},
and \code{UpstreamAsks} shrinks to \code{mkAppsInv} and \code{indSpineInj}.
\code{doc/rework/07-STATUS.md} section 4 specifies it; \stOpen{}.
\item \lead{Fourteen binders} \code{P}, \code{E}, \code{A}, \code{htbl}, \code{hsafe}, \code{hblk},
\code{hcfg}, \code{hcb}, \code{hwt}, \code{hsup}, \code{hprep}, \code{hrun}, \code{hwf},
\code{hbridge}. Checked terms discharge \code{hcfg}, \code{hwt}, \code{hsup} and \code{hwf} at all
eight instances, \code{hcb} at the first; the rest are hypotheses. \code{P} is taken at every level
scope.
\item \lead{\code{hbridge}} \stAssumed{}: the two environment fields of \code{ErasureBridge}, a binder
at the capstone and at every rung. \code{bridgeEnv\_of\_regContent} derives them from the run's
final state but has no consumer; no theorem produces its antecedents at a run of the whole eraser.
\item \lead{Eight instances} \code{Green.green\_G1} through \code{green\_G8}, each a real
\code{\#erase} run with a committed emitted program whose conclusion ends in a literal Peano numeral.
Each elaborates, and \code{lake exe green-check --all} passes: evidence that checked terms meet the
stated interface, not that anything inhabits it.
\item \lead{Peregrine's precondition, concluded} \code{LBWfPeregrine} carries MetaRocq's
\code{expanded\_tFix} as its \code{expandedFix} clause, decided by the Boolean checker at every
rung: the eraser registers a recursive body $\eta$-expanded (\code{LBTerm.etaFix}), so the fixpoint
node occurs under a long enough spine, every member body is $\lambda$-headed, and every
self-reference carries an argument.
\end{itemize}

\section{The architecture of the proof}
\label{sec:intro:architecture}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \lead{Target and semantics} (Chapter~\ref{chap:lambdabox}). \code{LBTerm} is MetaRocq's
\code{EAst.term} plus a free-variable and a primitive-value constructor, no cofixpoints.
\WcbvEval{} re-formalises \code{EWcbvEval}, flag-parameterised, stated at the erasure flag point
and carried to the entry flag point peregrine declares for its input. \code{lbEval\_sound} turns
each instance's evaluation into a kernel computation.
\item \lead{Source side} (Chapter~\ref{chap:source}). Every typing, definitional-equality and
translation fact is \code{lean4lean}'s. \SEval{} sits on top, its $\delta$ arm reading the
compiler-body table, with subject reduction \code{SEval.defeq}. The eraser's fragment is
\code{Supported}, checker \code{supportedB\_sound}.
\item \lead{Erasure specification} (Chapter~\ref{chap:erases}). \Erasable{} is [JACM]'s
\code{isErasable}. \Erases{}, [JACM, Fig. 18]: eleven rules, ten congruences plus one
non-deterministic box rule, with the image's binder names quantified, so an $\alpha$-variant of a
source term has the same erasures (\code{Erases.alpha}). \ErasesEnv{}, [JACM]'s \code{erases\_deps},
is one clause with seven conditions, of which the definition clause reads each tabled body at the
declaration's own level parameters; \code{Erases.instL} transports that reading to any
instantiation the reading scope knows.
\item \lead{Forward simulation} (Chapter~\ref{chap:correctness}). \code{erases\_correct}, [JACM]'s
theorem of the same name: eight binders carrying seven premises, one induction on the source
evaluation, the $\iota$, $\delta$ and projection arms as step lemmas. One simulation, on the
composite.
\item \lead{Lowering} (Chapter~\ref{chap:lowering}). \Lower{}, fifteen arms, relates two \lbox{}
terms. Carries the four Lean-specific steps: constructor constants to heads, saturated
\code{casesOn} spines to case nodes, block members to fixpoint nodes and to their $\eta$-expansion;
\LowerEnv{} prunes the environment. \code{LBWfPeregrine} (twelve clauses) is decided by
\code{lbWfPeregrine\_of\_check}.
\item \lead{Run model and refinement} (Chapters~\ref{chap:eraser}, \ref{chap:run},
\ref{chap:refinement}). \code{ErasureRun.lean} is the algebra of the eraser's monadic runs.
\code{visitExpr\_refines\_erasesLB} and its fixpoint companion say a successful run lands in
composite \code{ErasesLB} --- the analogue of [JACM]'s \code{erases\_erase} and [L, Lemma 11]. One
eighteen-motive fixpoint induction whose steps the capstone supplies as proved terms. A second
eighteen-motive bundle (\code{AccState}, \code{AccGrows}, \code{StepAcc1}--\code{StepAcc18},
\code{motives\_of\_steps\_acc}) threads $\Gamma_{\mathrm{spec}}$ as an accumulator; it is off the
capstone's proof path.
\item \lead{Cold start and bridge} (Chapter~\ref{chap:coldstart}). \code{erase\_run\_ok} splits the
entry point into preparation and term walk; \code{prepare\_sound} transports the source evaluation
across it; \code{erasure\_bridge\_of\_run} instantiates the bridge invariant at the empty entry
state and applies the refinement with all eighteen steps supplied, concluding the erasure half. It
cannot supply \code{ErasureBridge}'s two environment fields. \code{bridgeEnv\_of\_regContent}
derives them from \code{RegAcc}, \code{RegKeyed} and \code{ErasuresDeclared} at the final state;
\code{regInv\_registerInd\_run} and \code{regInv\_recConst\_step} preserve \code{RegAcc} across one
registration step.
\item \lead{Capstone and instances} (Chapter~\ref{chap:capstone}). The composition, plus the eight
instances: \code{erases\_correct} once at the spine, \code{firstorder\_erases\_core} for the
answer's constructor-tree shape and uniqueness, \code{noBox\_lower\_of\_foSpine} for box-freedom.
\end{enumerate}

Everything above is proved \emph{from} the hypotheses of Chapter~\ref{chap:trust}: the graph in this
blueprint shows the proofs, and the assumption nodes show where the proofs stop.

\section{What is open}
\label{sec:intro:open}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{The vacuity repair} \stOpen{} Until the five origin predicates read \code{env}'s own
declaration list, every rung, the capstone's observable conjunct and every accumulator step premised
on \code{AccAsks} are vacuous (Section~\ref{sec:intro:theorem}). \code{doc/rework/07-STATUS.md}
section 4 specifies the repair; \code{ConstOrigin.mono} and its three siblings do not survive it
unchanged.
\item \lead{\code{hbridge}} \stAssumed{} at the capstone and at all eight rungs.
\code{bridgeEnv\_of\_regContent} has no consumer, and \code{RegAcc} has producers only across one
registration step. The accumulator bundle proves seventeen of its eighteen steps; \code{StepAcc6}
(the mutual-block step) is \stOpen{}, and \code{AccGrows} has no producer. \code{StepAcc6} needs:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the block exit decomposed into one statement over its fresh variables and sibling exits;
\item a third eighteen-motive bundle proving the emitted term's constants declared in
$\Gamma_{\mathrm{spec}}$;
\item an \Erases{} induction showing every eliminator-spine prefix position of an erasure image is
$\Box$;
\item the constant-key entry layer, and the block exit's prefix produced from a run.
\end{enumerate}
Five of the seventeen proved steps hold only through the vacuity and need re-proof after the repair.
\code{doc/rework/07-STATUS.md} section 4 and \code{doc/rework/12-REPAIRS-W9.md} specify the work.
\item \lead{\code{lean4lean}} \stInherited{} and \stOpen{}. The pin proves the arity-inversion ask
(\code{VEnv.IsDefEqU.const\_arity\_inv}) and the constant-classification ask
(\code{VEnv.WF'.consts\_origin}), so neither is an \code{UpstreamAsks} field. The four fields are
\code{constsOrigin}, \code{constOriginExcludes} (refuted), \code{mkAppsInv} and \code{indSpineInj};
the last two wait on the fork. The compiler-body hypothesis \code{hcb} stays a binder at seven of
the eight rungs, blocked on the pin's unproven \code{TrProj.weak'\_inv} and \code{TrProj.uniq}.
\item \lead{\code{hfo}} \stOpen{} at every rung. Its discharge needs a fork fact joining the
inductive inversion with a declaration list (\code{TrEnv'.inductInfo\_wf'}), a specification-bundle
decision on which \code{find?} the model connection reads, and a length conjunct in
\code{firstOrderIndB}. Deriving it from \code{P} and \code{htbl} contradicts \code{A} until the
repair.
\item \lead{The specification bundles} \code{ErasureSpec}'s seven fields specify Lean
\code{Meta}/\code{Core} primitives no Lean term can state agreement with. Of \code{EraserAsks}'
four fields, the inductive-head verdict is not a theorem: a \emph{reduced} telescope longer than
the relevance check's budget, or any other kernel error inside it, routes the verdict to the
assumed \code{Meta} fallback.
\item \lead{Standing hypotheses} The source evaluation \code{hev} is constructed at G5 only
