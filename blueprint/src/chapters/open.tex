\section{Inherited sorries}\label{sec:open-inherited}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{L1, L2} sorry definitions of lean4lean for inductive declarations; no environment in
  scope contains one.
\item \lead{L3--L6} claims lean4lean \code{master} states and does not prove: adding an inductive
  keeps an environment ordered, sort and $\Pi$ injectivity, and no sort is definitionally equal
  to a $\Pi$-type. The final theorem depends on them (Section~\ref{sec:trust-inherited}).
\item \lead{Why open} proving them extends lean4lean, which is outside this verification.
\end{itemize}

\section{Shipping defects}\label{sec:open-shipping}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Reported, not fixed} the shipping-change register lists the defects it reports without
  fixing them (Section~\ref{reg:reported-not-fixed}).
\item \lead{Unrecorded dead code} \code{mkCase} in \code{LeanToLambdaBox/Erasure.lean} has no
  caller, and its result type names \code{EraseM}, which Lean binds as an auto-bound implicit; the
  register has no entry for it.
\item \lead{Peregrine on the pure path} of the 348 \code{validate} and \code{eval} checks on the
  173 in-fragment corpus outputs and NV-1's program, 10 fail: unescaped names (R-2), a fixpoint
  body that is not a $\lambda$ (R-11), and an evaluation that reaches an axiom (register entry
  S-21). Check C11 of \code{proof/scripts/check.sh} excludes exactly these 10.
\item \lead{Peregrine in CI} check C11 needs a Peregrine binary; the CI workflow skips it.
\end{itemize}
