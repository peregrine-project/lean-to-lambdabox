\section{What the final theorem does not say}\label{sec:open-notsay}

\begin{tabular}{p{0.3\linewidth}p{0.62\linewidth}}
\toprule
Topic & What the theorem leaves out \\
\midrule
Irrelevance of erased parts & If the eraser erases what the computation needs, the source evaluation
  is stuck; if it erases what con-leche types as data, or $\eta$-expands with a domain con-leche
  rejects, \code{hcert} fails. The theorem says nothing about such a program. \\
The oracle & \leandecl{Erasure.Pure.isErasable} is not verified. It defines the marks, the statements read
  it through \code{MarkOf}, and \code{hcert} checks each of its answers on the given program. \\
Boxes & A $\Box$ paired with a mark stands for a subterm con-leche types as a proof or a type former.
  A type-former certificate is the checker's verdict only. A $\Box$ of a recursor fixpoint's direct
  mode or index placeholders rests on declaration conditions. A $\Box$ of the value under a binder
  is a certified mark with evaluated values substituted, which no check repeats. \\
Partial correctness & The source is assumed to evaluate; nothing says that a typed program
  evaluates. \\
Type preservation & \code{Sound} says nothing when the input denotes the proof point. It speaks of
  meanings: \code{Nat.zero}, \code{Empty} and \code{PUnit.unit} share one denotation. \\
Model existence & The meaning claim holds in every con-leche set model. Models exist under con-leche's
  hypothesis on inaccessible cardinals, which this work does not use. \\
The con-leche binary & Acceptance is by con-leche's pure fold on our reading of the declarations,
  without \code{Nat.div} and \code{Nat.mod} pins. The binary's fold on exported declarations is
  compared with it only on the test programs. \\
Intermediate redexes & Every redex or data value that an evaluation step forms from evaluated parts
  must be typed by con-leche. Programs with an untypable intermediate redex, such as
  sort-polymorphic code used at \code{Prop}, are outside. \\
Stuck sources & Opaques, theorems and \code{@[extern]} atoms under \code{.preferAxiom} are not
  unfolded, a recursor is not reduced on a proof major, and computational \code{Classical.choice}
  does not evaluate. \\
Compiled code & The printer, the elaborator, the inlinings sidecar and con-leche's compiled
  \code{.olean} files are trusted. \\
Output & The typed $\lambda_\Box$ output, the backends and Peregrine. \\
Excluded programs & Chapter~\ref{chap:scope}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Non-vacuity gap} \hl{con-leche's acceptance of a program with an inductive type, and any
  certificate that needs $\iota$ or a non-trivial level comparison, is checked by compiled
  evaluation, not by the kernel}: the fold installs an inductive block through hash maps keyed by an
  opaque hash, and $\iota$ and level comparison use well-founded functions. Only inductive-free
  programs are kernel-checked end to end.
\end{itemize}
