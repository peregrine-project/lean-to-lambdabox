\section{Results}\label{sec:open-results}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Well-formed output} \stOpen{} fresh kernames, declaration order and well-formed terms
  of the emitted program, which composing with Peregrine's verified pipeline needs, as a conjunct
  or a separate theorem; \code{peregrine validate} checks them at run time (C11).
\item \lead{Non-vacuity} \stOpen{} a check of NV-K's lemma against the kernel's.
\item \lead{Recursors on proofs, \code{Quot.ind}} \stOpen{} \code{SrcEval} has no rule for a
  recursor whose major is a proof other than a constructor value (\code{True.rec}, \code{And.rec}
  on a theorem), nor for \code{Quot.ind}: programs that evaluate them are covered vacuously
  (DV-24, DV-29, DV-30).
\end{itemize}
