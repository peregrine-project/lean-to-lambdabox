\section*{At a glance}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Goal} State what is proved, what is trusted without proof, what a result inherits from

\item \lead{Main results} The model side, the source and target semantics, erasability and the
  erasure relation with their lemmas, soundness of the pure oracle, the relation-level simulation
  \code{erases\_correct}, and the function-level lemma \code{erasePure\_erases} about the shipping
  traversal, and \hl{the final theorem \code{erase\_correct} about \code{\#erase}}.
\item \lead{Status} \stProved{} every node that is not shipping code; \stInherited{} the lean4lean
  sorries that some of them reach; \stShipping{} the shipping code, described by definition
  nodes.
\item \lead{Lean files} \code{proof/EraseProof/} (the verification library \code{EraseProof});
  \code{LeanToLambdaBox/\{Basic, Erasure, Printing\}.lean} and
  \code{LeanToLambdaBox/Erasure/\{Collect, Pure, Entry, Command\}.lean} (the shipping code).
\end{itemize}

\section{Trusted, not verified}\label{sec:trust-trusted}

The final theorem (Chapter~\ref{chap:final}) speaks about the pure path of \code{\#erase} on the
kernel term. The following are outside every statement, as MetaRocq trusts its quoting.

\begin{tabular}{p{0.3\linewidth}p{0.62\linewidth}}
\toprule
Trusted part & What it does \\
\midrule
Elaboration & produces the kernel term \code{e} of \code{\#erase t} \\
\code{EnvView.ofEnvironment} & reads the Lean environment, including which constants are
  \code{@[extern]} (Definition~\ref{def:Erasure-EnvView}) \\
The printer and the file writes & \code{Serialize}, and the writes of \code{.ast},
  \code{.ast.inlinings} and \code{.mli} (Definitions~\ref{def:Serialize},
  \ref{def:Erasure-eraseElab}) \\
The inlining sidecar & the constants \code{.ast.inlinings} asks Peregrine to inline \\
Peregrine & compiles the emitted program \\
The \code{Meta} path & out-of-fragment inputs keep the shipping traversal with the elaborator's
  oracle and Lean's compiler passes (Definitions~\ref{def:Erasure-isErasable},
  \ref{def:Erasure-prepareErasure}) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Partial correctness} lean4lean has no normalization theorem: nothing is claimed when
  the eraser fails or the source does not evaluate.
\item \lead{Kernel terms} the theorem is about the kernel term, not Lean's compiled code: the
  eraser uses logical definitions, not \code{@[implemented\_by]} implementations (R-13), and
  emits opaque bodies that the source semantics does not unfold.
\item \lead{Stuck evaluations} an evaluation that reaches a computational axiom, a non-atom opaque
  or a remapped \code{@[extern]} constant has no source value, and the theorem says nothing about
  it.
\end{itemize}

\section{The shipping code}\label{sec:trust-shipping}

The eraser is Lean meta-code. Its traversal is total, bounded by fuel, and generic over a backend
monad. \code{\#erase} runs it with the pure backend on programs in the fragment
(Chapter~\ref{chap:pure}), and with the \code{CoreM} backend, which asks the elaborator for types,
on the others. The pure path uses lean4lean's pure functions \code{Expr.instantiate1'},
\code{Expr.liftLooseBVars'} and \code{Level.hasMVar'} (module \code{Lean4Lean.Verify.Axioms}),
and no shipping node reaches an axiom of that module.

\begin{definition}[Erased terms]
  \label{def:LBTerm}
  \lean{LBTerm}
  \leanok
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Basic.lean}{87}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} The $\lambda_\Box$ terms the eraser builds: MetaRocq's
  \code{EAst.term} (\code{erasure/theories/EAst.v}) with \hl{free variables added}.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{box}, \code{bvar}, \code{lambda}, \code{letIn}, \code{app}, \code{const},
        \code{construct}, \code{case}, \code{proj}, \code{fix}, \code{prim}: the MetaRocq
        constructors \code{tBox}, \code{tRel}, \code{tLambda}, \code{tLetIn}, \code{tApp},
        \code{tConst}, \code{tConstruct}, \code{tCase}, \code{tProj}, \code{tFix}, \code{tPrim};
  \item \code{fvar}: a Lean free variable, used during erasure and abstracted to \code{bvar}
        before a binder is built;
  \item absent: \code{tVar}, \code{tEvar}, \code{tCoFix}, \code{tLazy}, \code{tForce}.
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Abstraction of a free variable]
  \label{def:abstract}
  \lean{abstract, toBvar}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:LBTerm}
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Basic.lean}{143}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} \code{abstract x t} replaces the free variable \code{x} by the
  bound variable of the binders crossed, \hl{without shifting} other indices; \code{toBvar} is
  its structural recursion.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Global declarations]
  \label{def:GlobalDecl}
  \lean{GlobalDecl, ConstantBody, MutualInductiveBody, OneInductiveBody, ConstructorBody,
    ProjectionBody}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:LBTerm}
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Basic.lean}{188}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} The declarations of an erased environment.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{constantDecl}: a constant with an optional body (none for an axiom);
  \item \code{inductiveDecl}: a block of mutual inductive types: parameter count, and per type
        its name, propositional flag, allowed eliminations, constructors and projections.
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Erased programs]
  \label{def:ASTType}
  \lean{ASTType, Program}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:LBTerm, def:GlobalDecl}
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Basic.lean}{196}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} What \code{\#erase} emits: \code{untyped} of a list of named global
  declarations and an optional main term, the \code{Untyped} program of Peregrine's format.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Erasure configuration]
  \label{def:Erasure-ErasureConfig}
  \lean{Erasure.ErasureConfig, Erasure.Config.Extern, Erasure.Config.Nat}
  \leanok
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Erasure.lean}{68}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} The options of \code{\#erase ... config}.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{extern}: an \code{@[extern]} constant becomes an axiom (\code{preferAxiom}, the
        default) or keeps its Lean definition (\code{preferLogical});
  \item \code{nat}: \code{Nat} literals become primitive integers (\code{machine}, the default) or
        constructor terms (\code{peano});
  \item \code{csimp}, \code{remove\_irrel\_constr\_args},
        \code{auto\_inline\_typeclass\_dispatch}: Boolean options.
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Traversal context and names]
  \label{def:Erasure-TravCtx}
  \lean{Erasure.TravCtx, Erasure.Local, Erasure.binderNameOf, Erasure.fixDefName}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:Erasure-ErasureConfig}
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Erasure.lean}{172}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} The traversal's reader context, and the $\lambda_\Box$ names of
  binders and fixpoint members.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{TravCtx}: a Lean local context \code{lctx}, the same binders as a list
        \code{locals} of \code{Local}s (innermost first), the fixpoint variables in scope, the
        configuration;
  \item \code{binderNameOf n}: \code{n} if it is printable ASCII, anonymous otherwise (DV-15);
  \item \code{fixDefName n}: the name of the fixpoint member for the constant \code{n}.
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Backends and the erasure monad]
  \label{def:Erasure-EraseT}
  \lean{Erasure.EraseT, Erasure.Backend, Erasure.ErasureState, Erasure.run}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:GlobalDecl, def:Erasure-TravCtx}
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Erasure.lean}{238}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} $\mathrm{EraseT}\ m = $ \code{StateT ErasureState (ReaderT TravCtx
  m)} over \hl{a backend monad \code{m}}.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{Backend m}: the environment and type operations the traversal uses (constant
        lookup, fresh variables, instantiation, erasability, type inference, \code{casesOn} and
        constructor information, \code{@[extern]} and inline attributes, preprocessing, logging,
        the out-of-fuel error);
  \item the \code{CoreM} instance calls the Lean functions the traversal uses on the \code{Meta}
        path: \code{Environment.find?}, \code{Meta.inferType}, \code{isErasable}, and others;
  \item \code{ErasureState}: the registered inductives and constants, the emitted declarations,
        the constants to inline and their inlined sizes.
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Erasability oracle of the \texorpdfstring{\code{Meta}}{Meta} path]
  \label{def:Erasure-isErasable}
  \lean{Erasure.isErasable}
  \leanok
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Erasure.lean}{254}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} A term is erased to $\Box$ iff its type is a proposition or a
  type former, as \hl{decided by the elaborator}.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \lead{Lean} a \code{MetaM} function calling \code{Meta.inferType}, \code{Meta.isProp} and
        \code{Meta.isTypeFormerType}; the \code{CoreM} backend's \code{isErasable};
  \item \lead{Caveat} these belong to Lean's elaborator (\code{Lean.Meta}), a different program
        from the kernel that lean4lean models.
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Preprocessing]
  \label{def:Erasure-prepareErasure}
  \lean{Erasure.prepareErasure}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:Erasure-ErasureConfig}
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Erasure.lean}{300}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} The \code{CoreM} backend's \code{prepare}: rewrites the input term
  with passes of Lean's compiler before erasure.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \lead{Steps} unsafe-recursion names replaced, \code{macroInline}, \code{inlineMatchers},
        \code{macroInline} again, then (option \code{csimp}) the \code{@[csimp]} replacements;
  \item \lead{Caveat} the result may be ill-typed when a type depends on a function that
        \code{csimp} replaces (its docstring).
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The eraser]
  \label{def:Erasure-erase}
  \lean{Erasure.erase, Erasure.visitExpr, Erasure.travFuel}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:LBTerm, def:abstract, def:ASTType, def:Erasure-EraseT, def:Erasure-isErasable,
    def:Erasure-prepareErasure}
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Erasure.lean}{960}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} Erases a term and its dependencies to a program by a
  \hl{fuel-bounded, generic traversal}; \code{erase} runs it with the \code{CoreM} backend.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{erase e config}: runs the backend's \code{prepare}, then \code{visitExpr travFuel}
        in \code{EraseT CoreM}; returns the program (the emitted declarations and the main term)
        and the constants to inline;
  \item \code{visitExpr}: an erasable term becomes $\Box$; otherwise one case per \code{Expr}
        constructor, through the mutually recursive functions of the traversal
        (\code{visitApp}, \code{visitConst}, \code{visitMutual}, and others);
  \item \code{travFuel} $= 2^{32}$: every function of the traversal takes fuel and fails with
        the backend's \code{outOfFuel} at zero.
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Printing]
  \label{def:Serialize}
  \lean{Serialize, sexpr, LBTerm.to_sexpr}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:LBTerm, def:ASTType}
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Printing.lean}{15}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} The S-expression printer of programs, in Peregrine's \code{.ast}
  format.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{Serialize}: a class with one method, \code{to\_sexpr}, to the S-expressions
        \code{sexpr};
  \item \code{LBTerm.to\_sexpr}: a \code{partial def}; it reaches \code{unreachable!} on
        \code{fvar}.
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The command \texorpdfstring{\code{\#erase}}{\#erase}]
  \label{def:Erasure-eraseElab}
  \lean{Erasure.eraseElab, Erasure.erasestx}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:Erasure-eraseEntry, def:Erasure-EnvView, def:Serialize, def:Erasure-ErasureConfig}
  \srcloc{LeanToLambdaBox/Erasure/Command.lean}{18}
  \stShipping{} \lead{In short} Elaborates the term, erases it with \hl{\code{eraseEntry} over
  \code{EnvView.ofEnvironment}}, and writes the program, the constants to inline and the
  \code{.mli} signature: to files when \code{to} and \code{mli} are given, to the message log
  otherwise.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item the \code{config} term is evaluated with \code{unsafe Elab.Term.evalTerm};
  \item the \code{.mli} signature comes from the Lean type of the term (\code{gen\_mli});
  \item an error of the pure path stops the command with \code{erasure failed: ...}
        (Definition~\ref{def:Erasure-eraseEntry}).
  \end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Caveat} A green shipping node means that its declarations exist and use standard axioms
  only. It says nothing about their behaviour.
\item \lead{Caveat} The \code{CoreM} backend runs the elaborator's type inference and Lean's
  compiler passes; they are outside every Lean statement, and only out-of-fragment programs reach
  them.
\item \lead{Caveat} A \code{panic!} or \code{unreachable!} of the eraser returns a default value and
  continues (register entries R-4 and R-5, Chapter~\ref{chap:shipping-changes}).
\end{itemize}
