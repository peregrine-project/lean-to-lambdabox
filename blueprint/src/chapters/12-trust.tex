\chapter{The trust boundary: assumptions, axiom footprint, inherited sorries, findings, coverage, what is open}
\label{chap:trust}

The preceding eleven chapters state what the development proves. This one states what it does
not. The capstone \code{shipping\_erase\_correct\_firstorder}
(\code{LeanToLambdaBox/Capstone.lean}) is a \emph{conditional} theorem: fifteen explicit binders
stand in front of its conclusion, and at the eight green rungs only five of them --- six at G1
--- are replaced by checked terms. Three of the fifteen are bundles of further hypotheses ---
\code{ErasureSpec} (eight fields), \code{EraserAsks} (five fields) and \code{UpstreamAsks} (four
fields), declared in \code{LeanToLambdaBox/ErasureSpec.lean} and
\code{LeanToLambdaBox/Upstream.lean} --- and a fourth, \code{hbridge}, is an implication whose
consequent is a two-field bundle \code{ErasureBridge} that no theorem in the repository inhabits,
at any rung. Underneath all of that sits trust inherited from outside: sixteen \code{sorry} sites
in the pinned lean4lean fork, and twenty-nine non-standard axioms of Lean's own kernel-reflection
layer that the oracle reroute drags in. This chapter gives one node to each of those assumptions,
and then records the measured axiom footprint of every tracked theorem, the measured coverage of
the five benchmark programs and the eight rungs, the eleven shipping findings the verification
uncovered and deliberately did not fix, and what remains open.

The repository's own accounting is \code{doc/trust.md} (five classes A--E, a row per assumption),
\code{doc/rework/07-STATUS.md} (the class table, the footprint, the verdicts) and
\code{doc/coverage.md}, which \code{lake exe coverage} writes from the tree and re-checks in CI
with \code{lake exe coverage --check}. The numbers below are transcribed from those documents and
from the committed fixtures \code{test/ledger.expected} (40 \code{\#print axioms} rows in 368
lines) and \code{test/lean4lean-sorries.expected} (the pinned revision plus sixteen \code{sorry}
sites); every one of them is re-measured by a CI step, so a drifting claim shows up as a fixture
diff rather than as prose rot. What a fixture cannot catch is stated as such: \code{\#print
axioms} measures a \emph{proved term} and never a hypothesis, which is exactly why moving an
obligation from a bundle field to a named binder moves no row in the ledger.

How to read the nodes below. An \emph{assumption} node carries the Lean name of the structure
field that states it, when a Lean declaration does, and never a proof: in the dependency graph it
renders as stated-but-unproved, and every result depending on it inherits that colour. Nodes for
hypotheses stated inline in the capstone or a rung, and the two nodes with no Lean counterpart at
all, name instead the definition node of the predicate they mention. Two of the assumptions,
Assumption~\ref{asm:EraserAsks-kernel_ind_head_true} and
Assumption~\ref{asm:EraserAsks-block_keys_distinct}, are \emph{refuted in general} and hold only
on the measured inputs; every statement taking a bundle \code{E : EraserAsks} --- the capstone and
all eight rungs --- is bounded by them.

\section{The five trust classes}
\label{sec:trust:classes}

\code{doc/trust.md} grades every assumption on one scale, used by name in chapters
\ref{chap:erases}, \ref{chap:eraser}, \ref{chap:refinement}, \ref{chap:coldstart} and
\ref{chap:capstone} and defined here once.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cp{0.78\textwidth}}
\textbf{class} & \textbf{meaning} \\
\hline
\textbf{A} & proved here, measured footprint within \code{propext}, \code{Classical.choice},
\code{Quot.sound} \\
\textbf{B} & proved here, footprint additionally contains \code{sorryAx} inherited from
lean4lean --- in the ledger, \code{SEval.defeq} \\
\textbf{C} & a hypothesis of a stated theorem, visible in the statement a reader checks \\
\textbf{D} & a permanent named binder, mechanised outside Lean \\
\textbf{E} & a scope restriction or a fact about a consumer, stated and not formalised \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Every statement of this development is made at one pinned erasure configuration,
\code{ConfigPinned} (Definition~\ref{def:ConfigPinned}), whose five conjuncts are
\code{csimp = false}, \code{extern = .preferLogical}, \code{nat = .peano},
\code{remove\_irrel\_constr\_args = false} and
\code{auto\_inline\_typeclass\_dispatch = false}. \textbf{This is not the shipping default}: the
\code{ErasureConfig} the \code{\#erase} command uses when the user says nothing ships with
\code{csimp} on, \code{extern := .preferAxiom} and machine \code{Nat}, so it differs from the
pinned configuration on three of the five fields. A default \code{\#erase} invocation is outside
every theorem in this blueprint.

The class-\textbf{E} rows are scope restrictions, stated and not formalised. In the numbering of
the development's restriction register they are: N1, the \code{@[csimp]} replacement walk; N2,
\code{@[extern]} axiomatisation (a program reaching an \code{@[extern]} constant fails
\code{NoBodylessRefs}, Definition~\ref{def:NoBodylessRefs}, and is outside the domain); N3,
machine \code{Nat}; N4, the constructor argument mask; N5, auto-inlining and the
\code{.ast.inlinings} sidecar channel; N7, termination --- there is no source strong
normalisation result here, and the capstone keeps the conditional form of [S]; N10,
serialisation; N11, the remaining sidecars; N14, size; N16, \code{Quot}; N18, elimination out of
\code{Prop}. Three further rows are facts about consumers: peregrine's own precondition
obligation for its untyped transforms is \code{Admitted} and its \code{validate} verb checks no
expandedness; MetaRocq's shipped \code{firstorder\_ind} predicate is \code{false} on \code{nat},
so this development cites [S] and does not transcribe it; and no Rocq-side copy of $\Erases$ or
$\Lower$ exists, \code{doc/rules-Erases.md} and \code{doc/rules-Lower.md} being the anchor
instead.

\section{Inherited trust}
\label{sec:trust:inherited}

\begin{assumption}[The lean4lean trust boundary: sixteen inherited sorry sites]
\label{asm:lean4lean-trust}
\uses{def:lean4lean-grounding}
Everything this development proves about \emph{source} terms is relative to lean4lean's
metatheory, and the pinned fork is not \code{sorry}-free. The pin is
\code{20ec229f1a8c6358f3b3852c4e27d2be523d1b87}, the head of the \code{trproj} branch of a fork

\end{assumption}

\begin{remark}
Nothing in this repository can discharge Assumption~\ref{asm:lean4lean-trust}; it dies only with a
re-pin whose fixture diff is then inspected. The fixture's declaration names are attributed by a
line-oriented heuristic, so the \emph{sites} are the hard data and the names informational; and
sixteen is the package's complete inventory, six the subset reaching this tree. One consequence of
the boundary is measured rather than assumed: the projection arm of the simulation,
\code{step\_proj} (Theorem~\ref{thm:step_proj}), has \emph{exactly} the \code{sorryAx} roots of
the iota arm, name for name, and the two open projection lemmas of the executable checker do not
appear at all --- because it \emph{consumes} a \code{TrProj} witness from the premise of
\code{TrExprS.proj} and never builds one. The \code{TrProj} exposure at a projection is therefore
an \emph{inhabitation} problem, not an axiom problem: a projection in a real program has a
\code{TrExprS} derivation only through \code{inferProj.WF}, \code{sorry} at the pin.
\end{remark}

\section{Class C: what the eraser asks of itself}
\label{sec:trust:eraser}

\code{EraserAsks lenv env Us gw} (Definition~\ref{def:EraserAsks}) bundles what the correctness
statement assumes about code \emph{this repository owns} --- the \code{CoreM} passes of
\code{Erasure.prepare\_erasure} and \code{Erasure.isErasable}, the relevance oracle. Each of its
five fields is therefore an obligation with an owner whose honest end state is a proof, not a
specification of an input. The bundle is a binder of \code{erasure\_bridge\_of\_run}, of the
capstone and of every rung, and \textbf{no rung discharges any field}. A reader of
\code{doc/rework/07-STATUS.md} should note the arithmetic slip there: its prose says ``four
fields'' and then names five; the code has exactly five, plus one \emph{derived theorem},
\code{EraserAsks.oracle\_informative} (Theorem~\ref{thm:EraserAsks-oracle_informative}), proved
from \code{oracle\_false\_refl} and \code{kernel\_ind\_head\_true}.

\begin{assumption}[The preparation passes only advance the name generator]
\label{asm:EraserAsks-passes_monotone}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.EraserAsks.passes_monotone}
\leanok
\uses{def:preparePasses, def:Erasure-EraseM}
For each of the \code{CoreM} passes $f$ in \code{preparePasses} ---
\code{Erasure.replaceUnsafeRecNames}, \code{Lean.Compiler.LCNF.macroInline} and
\code{Lean.Compiler.LCNF.inlineMatchers} --- any run of $f$ lifted into the erasure monad takes
the ambient name generator, read out of \code{IO.RealWorld} by the reading function $gw$, to one
that is at least as advanced. The Lean field is \code{EraserAsks.passes\_monotone}; it quantifies
over membership in \code{preparePasses} rather than over the literal call sequence, which matters
because \code{Erasure.prepare\_erasure} calls the three passes four times, running
\code{macroInline} twice, and because the \code{@[csimp]} walk is out of scope by
\code{ConfigPinned}. Owner: this repository; discharged nowhere, at any rung; the route on record
is unfolding the generator discipline of \code{Lean.Core.transform} at the three passes.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[The preparation passes preserve the source evaluation, under any spine]
\label{asm:EraserAsks-passes_sound}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.EraserAsks.passes_sound}
\leanok
\uses{def:preparePasses, def:SEval}
For each pass $f$ of \code{preparePasses} and each run taking $e$ to $e'$: whenever the source
evaluation relates $\mkApps\ e\ args$ to a value $v$, it relates $\mkApps\ e'\ args$ to the same
$v$ --- for \emph{every} argument spine, compiler-body table, level scope, flag set and local
context. The Lean field is \code{EraserAsks.passes\_sound}. The spine is quantified because the
capstone reads its observable at $\mkApps\ e\ args$ while a pass is a whole-tree
\code{Lean.Core.transform} walk, and $f\,(\mkApps\ e\ args)$ is not $\mkApps\ (f\,e)\ args$, so a
clause stated at the subject alone would not reach the observable. The field is a binder at every
rung and discharged nowhere; what \emph{is} proved is its composition over the four calls,
\code{prepare\_sound} (Theorem~\ref{thm:prepare_sound}), which carries the source evaluation from
the subject to the prepared term at the spine and which the capstone spends. The route on record
is a delta-expansion argument for \code{macroInline} and a renaming argument for
\code{replaceUnsafeRecNames}.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[A false oracle verdict reflects the pure kernel run]
\label{asm:EraserAsks-oracle_false_refl}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.EraserAsks.oracle_false_refl}
\leanok
\uses{def:Erasure-isErasable, def:isErasable}
If the shipping oracle \code{Erasure.isErasable} (Definition~\ref{def:Erasure-isErasable}, the
\code{MetaM} function the eraser calls), lifted into the erasure monad, returns \code{false} at
the ambient level scope, then the \emph{pure} verified run of \code{LeanToLambdaBox.isErasable}
(Definition~\ref{def:isErasable}) at the same local context and scope did not answer \code{true}.
The Lean field is \code{EraserAsks.oracle\_false\_refl}; it states the pure run through
\code{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.run} at the kernel environment of \code{lenv}, at safety
\code{.safe}. A binder at every rung, discharged nowhere. It is near-definitional --- it is the
success arm of \code{Erasure.isErasable} modulo the environment and local-context reads --- and a
\code{MetaM} reflection lemma for that arm would discharge it.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[At an inductive-type head the kernel oracle answers true --- refuted in general]
\label{asm:EraserAsks-kernel_ind_head_true}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.EraserAsks.kernel_ind_head_true}
\leanok
\uses{def:IndInfo, def:isErasable, def:lean4lean-grounding}
If the head of $e$ is a constant that the model declares as an inductive type former, and $e$ has
a \code{TrExprS} translation at a well-formed modelled local context whose free variables the
kernel's own name generator reserves, then the pure verified run of
\code{LeanToLambdaBox.isErasable} at $e$ answers \code{true}. The Lean field is
\code{EraserAsks.kernel\_ind\_head\_true}; the reserving generator is
\code{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.kernelNGen}, a declaration this repository keeps in an
upstream-looking namespace pending an upstream ask. This is the oracle's \emph{completeness} at
the one shape the fragment cannot exclude, and it is what
\code{EraserAsks.oracle\_informative} composes with
Assumption~\ref{asm:EraserAsks-oracle_false_refl} to rule out an inductive-type head after a
\code{false} verdict.

The field is a binder at every rung, discharged nowhere --- and it is \textbf{false} in general,
at a \code{forall}-telescope deep enough to exhaust the fuel: \code{LeanToLambdaBox.isArityCheck}
takes its fuel as the approximate depth of the type plus one, and that depth is an eight-bit
field saturating at 255, so the fuel caps at 256 and is \textbf{1} at a definitional alias, where
the depth is zero (finding F-DEPTH). Measured the other way, the kernel run answers \code{true}
at 2{,}940 of 2{,}940 inductive type formers of the elaboration environment. A discharge needs
three executable-shape lemmas lean4lean does not have --- that \code{inferType} on a
constant-headed spine returns the instantiated declared telescope, that \code{whnf} is the
identity on a syntactic \code{forallE}, and that the fuel covers the telescope --- together with
F-DEPTH's fuel change.
\end{assumption}

\begin{remark}
Every statement that takes a bundle \code{E : EraserAsks} --- the capstone and all eight rungs
--- is bounded by Assumption~\ref{asm:EraserAsks-kernel_ind_head_true}, and
\code{EraserAsks.oracle\_informative} is exactly as strong, so the bound transfers to it and to
everything downstream of it.
\end{remark}

\begin{assumption}[A mutual block's lambda-box keys are distinct --- refuted in general]
\label{asm:EraserAsks-block_keys_distinct}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.EraserAsks.block_keys_distinct}
\leanok
\uses{def:Kername}
Whenever \code{Lean.Compiler.LCNF.getDeclInfo?} answers with a \code{ConstantInfo}, the list of
$\lbox$ keys obtained from that declaration's mutual block, after stripping the
\code{\_unsafe\_rec} companion suffix and applying \code{toKername}, has no duplicate. The Lean
field is \code{EraserAsks.block\_keys\_distinct}. A collapse here is a \emph{miscompilation}, not
a proof gap: \code{Erasure.visitMutual} builds the fixvar map from exactly that list and
\code{Erasure.mkDef} reads it back by name.

The field is a binder at every rung, discharged nowhere --- and it is \textbf{false} on a legal
\code{mutual unsafe def} block pairing a definition with its own \code{\_unsafe\_rec} companion,
which maps to one distinct key for two members, two fixpoint definitions with the same name, both
registered at one kername, and no error reported (finding F-UNSAFEREC). Measured: 0 failures over
228{,}937 \code{getDeclInfo?} answers. The one-line \code{Nodup} guard F-UNSAFEREC proposes would
turn the assumption into a run-recovered fact.
\end{assumption}

\section{Class D: the specification of Lean's primitives}
\label{sec:trust:spec}

\code{ErasureSpec lenv env Us gw} (Definition~\ref{def:ErasureSpec}) specifies the ambient
primitives the erasure runs on --- \code{Lean.Environment} lookups, the fresh-name supply, the
relevance oracle's plumbing, the kernel's inductive blocks --- at the elaboration environment
\code{lenv}, its model \code{env}, the level scope and the generator reading. Class \textbf{D}
throughout: each field is a permanent named binder about an object no Lean term denotes
(\code{IO.RealWorld} is opaque and \code{Lean.Environment} has a private constructor), mechanised
outside Lean where it can be mechanised at all. Three of its eight fields are partly \emph{earned}
rather than assumed, through the theorems \code{ErasureSpec.envWF},
\code{ErasureSpec.oracle\_sound\_of\_run} and \code{ErasureSpec.decl\_adequate\_of\_kernelFind};
the nodes below say where. A bundle \code{P : ErasureSpec lenv env [] gw} is a binder of the
capstone and of every rung.

\begin{assumption}[The elaboration environment is modelled by the specification environment]
\label{asm:ErasureSpec-env_connect}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.ErasureSpec.env_connect}
\leanok
\uses{def:lean4lean-grounding}
There is a family of model environments, well formed for the kernel environment underlying
\code{lenv}, of which \code{env} is the \code{.safe} member. The Lean field is
\code{ErasureSpec.env\_connect}; it names the kernel-environment conversion explicitly because
lean4lean's well-formedness is stated at \code{Lean.Kernel.Environment}, and it is the same
conversion the shipping oracle makes. A binder at every rung, discharged nowhere; upstream ask 4
would derive it. What it \emph{buys} is proved: \code{ErasureSpec.envWF}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:ErasureSpec-envWF}) derives the model's well-formedness from it, so the
target-side metatheory's well-formedness premise is earned rather than assumed, and the capstone
spends it twice.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Environment lookups report what the elaboration environment holds]
\label{asm:ErasureSpec-lookup_adequate}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.ErasureSpec.lookup_adequate}
\leanok
\uses{def:LookupAdequate, def:CasesOnShape}
The four environment queries a run makes --- \code{Lean.getConstInfo},
\code{Lean.Compiler.LCNF.getDeclInfo?}, \code{Lean.Compiler.LCNF.getCtorArity?} and
\code{Lean.getCasesInfo?} --- report what \code{lenv} holds and none of them advances the name
generator, in guarded or two-sided form. The Lean field is \code{ErasureSpec.lookup\_adequate},
and it is one application of the four-field predicate \code{LookupAdequate}
(Definition~\ref{def:LookupAdequate}): \code{constInfo}, that \code{getConstInfo} returns
\code{lenv}'s own declaration; \code{declInfo}, that the visited name lies in the block
\code{getDeclInfo?} answers with --- under the guard that the name is not an \code{\_unsafe\_rec}
companion, since the primitive prefers the companion and the membership is false at such a name
--- and that the query answers \code{none} only for a name \code{lenv} does not know, which is
what makes it total at a tabled head; \code{ctorArity}, that \code{getCtorArity?} answers exactly
for the constructors \code{lenv} declares and for no other name, the negative direction being the
constructor exclusion the refinement's fourth motive needs; and \code{casesInfo}, that
\code{getCasesInfo?} answers exactly for the \code{casesOn} constants, at metadata agreeing with
the declared block. A binder at every rung, discharged nowhere: it is the specification of four
opaque compiler queries, and no statement about \code{getConstInfo} can be discharged inside Lean.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Fresh free variables are fresh]
\label{asm:ErasureSpec-fresh_names}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.ErasureSpec.fresh_names}
\leanok

\code{Lean.mkFreshFVarId}, run in the erasure monad, returns an identifier the ambient generator
has not handed out, reserves it afterwards, only advances the generator, and returns an
identifier the kernel's own generator reserves. The Lean field is
\code{ErasureSpec.fresh\_names}, a four-way conjunction; the kernel generator it names is
\code{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.kernelNGen}, declared by \emph{this} repository inside an
upstream-looking namespace. A binder at every rung, discharged nowhere --- the generator is read
out of \code{IO.RealWorld}, which is opaque, so no Lean proof of this can exist in the present
formulation.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[The monadic oracle run reflects the kernel predicate]
\label{asm:ErasureSpec-oracle_refl}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.ErasureSpec.oracle_refl}
\leanok
\uses{def:Erasure-isErasable, def:isErasable, def:Oracle-MetaSound}

\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[The isErasableMeta fallback and the off-scope arm are sound]
\label{asm:ErasureSpec-oracle_meta}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.ErasureSpec.oracle_meta}
\leanok
\uses{def:Oracle-MetaSound, def:Erasure-isErasable}

checker concludes at the scope it ran in, while the consumer holds its translation witness at the
ambient scope, and a term that translates at one scope need not translate at another. At the
capstone's empty scope the field covers exactly the runs made below a polymorphic declaration.
A binder at every rung, discharged nowhere, and with no proved arm. The measurement on record ---
0 fallback hits in 139{,}196 constants --- is evidence about the \emph{error} route of
\code{Erasure.isErasable}, not about the scope-mismatch arm, which fires on a \emph{successful}
kernel run made below a polymorphic declaration.
\end{assumption}

\section{The four load-bearing upstream asks}
\label{sec:trust:upstream}

\code{UpstreamAsks env} (Definition~\ref{def:UpstreamAsks}) packages items 2, 6, 9 and 10 of
\code{doc/upstream-asks.md} as one structure, so that every consumer takes an identical,
auditable premise instead of a private one, and so that one pin bump discharges all four with no
change to any consumer's statement shape. It is taken by \code{erases\_correct} --- hence by
everything closed on it --- by \code{not\_erasable\_of\_informative}, \code{indSpine\_not\_prop},
\code{elim\_major}, \code{ctor\_saturated}, \code{CasesOnShape.agree},
\code{fOFields\_of\_asks}, \code{firstorder\_erases\_deterministic} and
\code{firstorder\_no\_box}; and \code{erasure\_bridge\_of\_run} takes it for three of the
bridge's eighteen steps, so \textbf{every rung carries it}. It is not measurable in the ledger:
\code{\#print axioms} reports the footprint of a proved term and never a hypothesis.

\begin{assumption}[Ask 2: the origin of a constant, and uniqueness of the declaring block]
\label{asm:UpstreamAsks-constsOrigin}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.UpstreamAsks.constsOrigin}
\leanok
\uses{def:CtorOf, def:IndInfo, def:ConstOrigin, def:CasesOnShape, def:IndBlockBelow}
Eight conjuncts, stated as the corollaries and the totality direction this tree consumes: a
constructor is not an inductive type name; \code{CtorOf}, \code{IndInfo} and \code{CasesOnShape}
are functional in their data (three conjuncts); every declared constant is a constructor, an
inductive type name or a plain constant; a plain constant is neither of the first two; an
\code{IndInfo} yields an \code{IndDeclOf}; and two well-formed declaration lists below \code{env}
that both declare the same inductive declare the \emph{same} block --- uniqueness at the
declaration level, stated in the below-\code{env} form \code{IndBlockBelow}. The Lean field is
\code{UpstreamAsks.constsOrigin}. The exclusion direction is what the uniqueness of the erasure
and the iota head step consume. A binder at every rung, discharged nowhere here; it dies with the
pin bump, against the fork's own constants-keyed origin lemma, after which the ambient
\code{UpstreamAsks} binder drops with no change of statement shape. Its first conjunct is
\emph{already} redundant --- \code{CtorOf.not\_indInfo} proves it here --- and is kept so that the
field grants everything ask 2 was filed for.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Ask 6: an inductive-type-former spine is neither a sort nor a Pi]
\label{asm:UpstreamAsks-constArityInv}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.UpstreamAsks.constArityInv}
\leanok
\uses{def:lean4lean-grounding, asm:lean4lean-trust}
For a well-formed declaration list containing an inductive block, a typed application headed by
one of its type formers is definitionally equal to neither a sort nor a Pi. The Lean field is
\code{UpstreamAsks.constArityInv}; it takes the list's well-formedness, the context's, and a
typing witness for the spine, and concludes the two negations. A binder at every rung, discharged
nowhere here. Its consumers in this tree are \code{not\_erasable\_of\_informative} directly, and
\code{fOFields\_of\_asks} through the Pi-spine peeling lemma. Its home upstream is beside
\code{sort\_inv}, \code{forallE\_inv\_stratified} and \code{sort\_forallE\_inv} --- all three
\code{sorry} at the pin and already inherited here --- so the expectation on record is that ask 6
lands the same way and that its \code{sorryAx} root then inherits into the iota and projection
arms of the simulation and into the uniqueness theorem.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Ask 9: spine typing inversion]
\label{asm:UpstreamAsks-mkAppsInv}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.UpstreamAsks.mkAppsInv}
\leanok
\uses{def:IndBlockBelow, def:lean4lean-grounding, asm:lean4lean-trust}
If an application spine has a type in a well-formed context, under the strong-order
well-formedness of the model, then its head has a type and the argument-by-argument \code{Peel}
relation holds: the head's type is definitionally a Pi whose domain types the first argument, and
the instantiated codomain peels the rest. The Lean field is \code{UpstreamAsks.mkAppsInv}, and it
makes the strong order an \emph{explicit} hypothesis so that the ask is \code{sorryAx}-free
exactly as the single-application inversion is. A binder at every rung, discharged nowhere here;
its only consumer in this tree is \code{indSpine\_not\_prop}, the other three candidates reaching
their spines through \code{TrExprS} and the peel lemma proved here instead. No lemma anywhere in
lean4lean's typing theory is about the type of a spine, and the forward half is about forty lines
from the single-application inversion plus uniqueness of types; what it does \emph{not} buy is
\code{sorry}-freedom, since the strong order's only introduction rests on
\code{VEnv.WF.patsStrong}, which is \code{sorry} at the pin.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Ask 10: definitionally equal inductive spines have the same head]
\label{asm:UpstreamAsks-indSpineInj}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.UpstreamAsks.indSpineInj}
\leanok
\uses{def:IndDeclOf, def:lean4lean-grounding, asm:lean4lean-trust}
Two definitionally equal application spines headed by \emph{inductively declared} type formers
have the same head. The Lean field is \code{UpstreamAsks.indSpineInj}. Its \code{IndDeclOf}
premises are load-bearing, not decoration: without them the statement is false, refuted by a
plain definition whose body is an inductive type. A binder at every rung, discharged nowhere
here; its one consumer is \code{fOFields\_of\_asks}. The expectation on record is that it lands
with the three injectivity \code{sorry}s and Church--Rosser, not before.
\end{assumption}

\section{The residual: the bridge's two environment fields}
\label{sec:trust:bridge}

What the capstone assumes \emph{beyond} the erasure half is one named binder, \code{hbridge}.
Read at the state the \code{visitExpr} run ends in, it says: there is a specification environment
$\Gamma_{\mathrm{spec}}$ adequate for that state, and for every $\lbox$ term that the prepared
subject erases to and that lowers to the emitted term, \code{ErasureBridge}
(Definition~\ref{def:ErasureBridge}) holds. That structure has exactly \textbf{two} fields --- a
design document still describing five is stale: the well-formedness field became the capstone's
own \code{hwf} binder, and the simulation and box-freedom fields were retired in the closing
round. The proved interface to both is \code{bridgeEnv\_of\_regInv}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:bridgeEnv_of_regInv}), which derives them from the registration invariant
\code{RegInvShape'} (Definition~\ref{def:RegInvShape-prime}) and \code{RegSaturated}
(Definition~\ref{def:RegSaturated}) at the run's final state --- but \textbf{nothing produces
that invariant there for a run of the shipping eraser}, so the bundle stands whole, at every rung.

\begin{assumption}[The specification environment erases the source environment]
\label{asm:ErasureBridge-erasesEnv}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.ErasureBridge.erasesEnv}
\leanok
\uses{def:ErasesEnv}
$\ErasesEnv$ holds of the specification environment along the closure of the erasure: every
constant, block and axiom the erased term reaches is declared in $\Gamma_{\mathrm{spec}}$ in the
way the environment-erasure relation demands. The Lean field is
\code{ErasureBridge.erasesEnv}. It is discharged \textbf{nowhere, at any rung}:
\code{RegInvShape'.erasesEnv} (Theorem~\ref{thm:RegInvShape-prime-erasesEnv}) derives it from the
registration invariant at the run's final state, at the three side conditions
\code{bridgeEnv\_of\_regInv} names --- but no theorem establishes that invariant for a run of the
shipping eraser. What would is the single content clause of the repair plan: a content predicate
carrying both readings of a \emph{single} erasure witness, read at a $\Gamma_{\mathrm{spec}}$
threaded as an \emph{output} of the refinement and grown with the state by a fresh-prefix
extension. Three measured obstructions stand in its way; see
Section~\ref{sec:trust:open}.
\end{assumption}

\begin{remark}
This is the single most important caveat of the whole blueprint: the crown theorem is a
composition whose environment half is assumed. No statement anywhere in this document may present
the capstone, or any rung, as unconditionally established.
\end{remark}

\begin{assumption}[The emitted environment is the lowered, pruned specification environment]
\label{asm:ErasureBridge-lowerEnv}
\lean{LeanToLambdaBox.ErasureBridge.lowerEnv}
\leanok
\uses{def:LowerEnv}
$\LowerEnv$ holds between the specification environment and the emitted one: the emitted
environment is the image of the specification environment under the lowering pass, pruned to what
the program reaches. The Lean field is \code{ErasureBridge.lowerEnv}. It is discharged nowhere,
at any rung, for the reason Assumption~\ref{asm:ErasureBridge-erasesEnv} is:
\code{RegInvShape'.lowerEnv} (Theorem~\ref{thm:RegInvShape-prime-lowerEnv}) derives it from the
same invariant at a saturated registry, and nothing inhabits that invariant.
\end{assumption}

\begin{remark}
\code{bridgeEnv\_of\_regInv} is \emph{proved}, at nine axioms and no \code{sorryAx}. The gap is
the inhabitation of its premises, not the implication; a reader must not mistake the proved
interface for a discharge.
\end{remark}

\begin{assumption}[G8 only: the subject's erasure reaches Nat's block]
\label{asm:hargReach}
\uses{def:ReachableFrom, def:ErasesEnv, def:Lower, def:LowerEnv, def:Erases}
At rung G8 alone: for every specification environment and every $\lbox$ term that the G8 subject
erases to, if that environment erases the source environment along it, lowers it to the committed
G8 term and lowers to the committed G8 environment, then the closure of the erasure in the
specification environment reaches the $\lbox$ key of \code{Nat}'s inductive block. The binder is
stated inline in the signature of \code{green\_G8}; no declaration states it. It is what remains
of the observable clause's per-argument $\ErasesEnv$ conjunct once \code{Green.g8\_argErasesEnv}
has transported the subject's environment clause to the argument's image.

A binder at G8 and nowhere else: G1--G7 apply the observable clause at the empty spine, where the
per-argument premise is vacuous. It is \emph{not} slack: at the empty specification environment
the conjunct is false --- the argument's image reaches the block key and the lookup answers
\code{none} --- while lowering a bare constant to itself still holds, which is exactly why the
binder carries the erasure and lowering clauses as antecedents rather than being stated at every
environment. It is true of any real run, since the environment-erasure relation at the tabled
body forces the specification environment to declare an erasure whose closure runs to
\code{Nat.succ} and \code{Nat.zero}; proving it needs the same content clause the two fields

\end{assumption}

\section{The standalone capstone and rung binders}
\label{sec:trust:binders}

Ten binders remain, stated inline in \code{shipping\_erase\_correct\_firstorder} and in the rung
theorems. The capstone's fifteen explicit binders are, in order, \code{P}, \code{E}, \code{A},
\code{htbl}, \code{hsafe}, \code{hblk}, \code{hcfg}, \code{hcb}, \code{hwt}, \code{hsup},
\code{hprep}, \code{hrun}, \code{hnb}, \code{hwf} and \code{hbridge}. Of these \code{hcfg},
\code{hwt}, \code{hsup}, \code{hnb} and \code{hwf} are checked terms at every rung --- by
\code{Green.spike\_configPinned}, \code{Witness.trExprS\_const\_of\_table},
\code{supportedB\_sound}, a kernel-reduced decision procedure, and
\code{lbWfPeregrine\_of\_check} on a kernel-reduced Boolean --- and are therefore not assumptions
of this chapter. The rung theorems add four further inline binders that the capstone
\emph{discharges by universal quantification} rather than assumes --- \code{hev}, \code{hvwt},
\code{hty} and \code{hfo}, the observable clause's own premises --- and G5 additionally binds two
fixture bundles and three definitional-equality witnesses, while G8 binds one fixture bundle and
\code{hargReach}. Counting off the sources: G1 has 13 binders, G2, G3, G4, G6 and G7 have 14, G5
has 18 (and no \code{hev}, which it proves), and G8 has 16.

Two facts about the capstone's signature belong here, because neither is recorded in the
repository's documents. First, \code{hnb} is an \textbf{unused binder}: the proof term never
mentions it, and \code{NoBodylessRefs} is absent from the conclusion's six non-observable
conjuncts --- yet the repository presents that predicate as the analogue of [S]'s
\code{axiom\_free} and as the honest exclusion for finding F-EQREC, a role the \emph{statement}
does not give it. Second, two conclusion conjuncts are literally the theorem's own hypotheses ---
\code{hprep}, re-exported verbatim, and \code{hwf}, passed straight through --- so neither may be
described as established by the theorem.

\begin{assumption}[The reified source table is the live environment's slice]
\label{asm:htbl}
\uses{def:Witness-SourceTableAdequate, def:Witness-SourceTable}
Every constant the reified table pins is pinned as \code{lenv} declares it, and its tabled body is
what \code{Erasure.prepare\_erasure} computes from the constant's compiler value; and every tabled
inductive type is pinned. The binder is \code{htbl : SourceTableAdequate lenv tbl}
(Definition~\ref{def:Witness-SourceTableAdequate}). Its body clause pins the compiler body only
\emph{up to} the reifier's alpha-equivalence --- an inductive relation blind to binder names and
binder info, and to nothing else --- deliberately, since on equality the clause is uninhabited
wherever \code{inlineMatchers} fires and draws \code{let} binder names from the name generator.
Class \textbf{D}: no Lean term denotes the ambient \code{Lean.Environment}.

A binder at the capstone and at all eight rungs, discharged nowhere in Lean, and mechanised
outside the kernel: \code{lake exe reify --check} compares the table field by field against the
live environment and cross-checks Lean's structural equality test against a Boolean written
arm-for-arm against the relation. It passes on all eight rung tables; at G7 and G8 it reports
five bodies matching only up to binder names --- \code{Nat.add}, \code{Nat.mul}, \code{Nat.pow},
\code{Nat.pred} and \code{Nat.sub}, the five whose preparation inlines a matcher --- and no
mismatch. The alpha-slack is one of the three measured obstructions of
Section~\ref{sec:trust:open}.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Every tabled declaration is safe, and the table's two columns agree]
\label{asm:hsafe}
\uses{def:TableSafe}
The binder is \code{hsafe : TableSafe lenv tbl} (Definition~\ref{def:TableSafe}), five clauses.
Three are safety clauses: every tabled constant, every tabled inductive type and every
constructor of the inductive column is \code{safe} in \code{lenv}. They exist because every
statement that puts a tabled name into the model reads it through
Assumption~\ref{asm:ErasureSpec-decl_adequate}, which is stated at safe declarations only, while
the table's adequacy predicate does not record the safety flag. Two further clauses are about the
table's own constant column: no tabled constant is an \code{\_unsafe\_rec} companion --- the
guard the \code{declInfo} clause of Assumption~\ref{asm:ErasureSpec-lookup_adequate} takes --- and
a tabled constant that \code{lenv} declares a constructor is in the table's constructor column
too, which is what \code{Erasure.visitConstApp} needs to read the fragment's saturation condition
at a constructor-arity hit. Class \textbf{D}, for the reason Assumption~\ref{asm:htbl} is.

A binder at the capstone and at all eight rungs, discharged nowhere. \code{lake exe reify --check}
reads the live declarations for the three safety clauses; the safety column itself is an upstream
ask. The two column clauses are true by construction --- the companion test is a pure function of
the name, and the reifier's constructor arm reifies the constructor's own inductive block --- but
no \code{reify} verb reads either, so they are unmechanised. The binder is load-bearing four
times: in \code{Green.g1\_compilerBodies}, in \code{supportedB\_sound}, and in two of the eighteen
bridge steps.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[The blocks the run installs are tabled, lambda-headed and informative]
\label{asm:hblk}
\uses{def:TableBlocks}
The binder is \code{hblk : TableBlocks lenv env tbl} (Definition~\ref{def:TableBlocks}), three
clauses about the blocks \code{Erasure.visitMutual} installs a fixvar map for: every member of an
installed block is itself tabled; every member's tabled body is a lambda, which is what makes the
emitted member body a lambda and the installed block a well-formed fixpoint; and no member is
erasable, stated as the negation the oracle's soundness contradicts. The third is a conjunct and
not a formality: \code{run\_mkDef\_box\_not\_lambda} exhibits an erasable member registered with a
non-lambda body. Class \textbf{D}.

A binder at the capstone and at all eight rungs, discharged nowhere --- and, more than that,
\textbf{consumed by no theorem in the chain}: the capstone forwards it to
\code{erasure\_bridge\_of\_run}, which passes it to none of the eighteen steps, and the only lemma
in the tree that takes \code{TableBlocks} at all, \code{blockKeyed\_install}, has no consumer of
its own, as its module's docstring records. \code{lake exe reify --blocks} checks the first two
clauses against the live environment; the third is model-side and no computation reaches it.
Measured over the thirteen tables --- the eight committed rung tables plus five reified on the
corpus entry constants --- 63 blocks install a fixvar map, 63 of them singletons, with no
untabled member and no non-lambda body. A blanket version of the second clause would be false on
every table: the non-lambda tabled bodies are the non-recursive instance constants and the rung
subjects themselves.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Every tabled compiler body is kernel-typeable at its declared type]
\label{asm:hcb}
\uses{def:CompilerBodies, def:lean4lean-grounding}
The binder is \code{hcb : CompilerBodies lenv env tbl.body?}
(Definition~\ref{def:CompilerBodies}): for every constant the table gives a body for, the
constant is declared in \code{lenv}, and the body translates and has the translation of the
constant's declared type --- both at the constant's own level scope and in the empty context.

It is a \textbf{checked term at G1 only}, where \code{Green.g1\_compilerBodies} builds it from
Assumptions~\ref{asm:ErasureSpec-env_connect}--\ref{asm:ErasureSpec-block_adequate},
\ref{asm:htbl} and \ref{asm:hsafe}; at G2--G8 it is a binder. Why it does not generalise: the
tables of G2--G4 carry the class projection \code{OfNat.ofNat}, whose typing needs a
\code{TrExprS} derivation through the \code{TrProj} layer, entirely unproven at the pin; G5 and
G6's carry an eliminator spine and a recursive body; and G7 and G8's Arith table carries ten
projection-bearing bodies among its thirty bodied declarations, with twenty-eight of its
forty-four declarations polymorphic, which the monomorphic G1 route does not reach. It is
checkable per program by running lean4lean's checker on each body-and-type pair; what blocks the
ladder is \code{TrProj} at the pin, the territory of upstream asks 3 and 4.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[The prepared term is the subject]
\label{asm:hprep}
\uses{def:Erasure-prepare_erasure, def:ConfigPinned}
The preparation run on the subject succeeds from the empty state and returns the prepared term
with the state unchanged. The binder is an equation stated inline in the capstone's signature; at
a rung it is instantiated with the prepared term equal to the subject, so that the preparation is
the \emph{identity} at the rung's own subject. Class \textbf{D}: \code{IO.RealWorld} is opaque,
so no Lean proof of a run's outcome exists.

A binder at the capstone and at all eight rungs, discharged nowhere in Lean, and mechanised
outside: \code{lake exe reify --prepared} runs the preparation against the live environment and
reports identity, green at all eight rung subjects --- the six spike constants and Arith's two.
It is load-bearing because the capstone's syntactic conjuncts read the \emph{prepared} term while
\code{Supported} and \code{TrExprS} are checked at the subject, and transporting those two across
the passes is a stronger statement about \code{macroInline} that is false in general.
\end{assumption}

\begin{remark}
The equation of Assumption~\ref{asm:hprep} is re-exported verbatim as the first conjunct of the
capstone's conclusion. That conjunct is never established by the theorem; it is carried.
\end{remark}

\begin{assumption}[The \#erase run produced the committed program]
\label{asm:hrun}
\uses{def:Erasure-erase, def:ConfigPinned}
The function \code{\#erase} calls, run on the subject at the pinned configuration from the
ambient world, returns the untyped program whose environment and entry term are the ones the
theorem names, together with a list of inlining hints that the statement binds and discards ---
the \code{.ast.inlinings} sidecar channel. The binder is an equation stated inline; at a rung the
environment and term are the \emph{committed} literals. Class \textbf{D}, and the canonical
permanent binder: \code{IO.RealWorld} is opaque, so no Lean proof of this can exist.

A binder at the capstone and at all eight rungs, discharged nowhere. It is mechanised outside the
kernel by \code{lake exe green-check}, which re-runs \code{\#erase} and byte-diffs the emitted

\section{What is open}
\label{sec:trust:open}

\textbf{The content clause.} Proof work in this repository is \emph{one} item: the two fields of
Assumptions~\ref{asm:ErasureBridge-erasesEnv} and~\ref{asm:ErasureBridge-lowerEnv}.
\code{bridgeEnv\_of\_regInv} is the proved interface; nothing produces the registration invariant
at the run's final state. The repair plan states what would: one content clause carrying both
readings of a \emph{single} erasure witness, read at a specification environment threaded as an
\emph{output} of the refinement and grown with the state by a fresh-prefix extension. That is
what closes the determinism gap --- the content predicate's definition clause names \emph{some}
erasure of the compiler body, while the invariant's definition field demands the emitted body be
the lowering image of \emph{that} one, and $\Erases$ is not deterministic outside the first-order
fragment --- and the plan also shows that the registry-domain converse is a theorem about the
run's constant extension, not a missing clause. Assumption~\ref{asm:hargReach} at G8 is the same
obligation seen from the ladder.

\textbf{Three measured obstructions} stand in the way, each independent, each a measurement rather
than an opinion. \emph{(i) The level scope.} The eighteen motives of the refinement are stated at
a fixed level scope, which the mutual-block pass re-enters under the member's own level
parameters, and 16 of G7's 30 tabled bodies belong to a polymorphic constant. \emph{(ii) Alpha.}
The table's body clause pins a tabled body only up to the reifier's alpha-equivalence ---
deliberately, since on equality the clause is uninhabited wherever the matcher inliner fires ---
while $\Lower$ is not alpha-closed on its source, its two fixpoint arms reading a declared body
out of the environment by equality; the reify checker reports five of G7 and G8's bodies matching
only up to binder names. \emph{(iii) The shape of the environment quantifier.} The refinement's
run predicate reads content at \emph{every} specification environment of the final state, where
the repair produces \emph{one}, and the two cannot be proved in one induction without restating
all eighteen motives. The alpha-transport kit that would have served (i) and (ii) --- 99
declarations --- was deleted in the closing round, on the rule that a file outside the import
closure earns an exemption only by naming a scheduled consumer.

\textbf{The upstream asks.} \code{doc/upstream-asks.md} is this side's register; editing the

\section*{Deviations from the paper, and caveats}

Nothing in this development is unconditional, and four caveats should travel with every claim
made from it.

\begin{itemize}
\item The capstone is a conditional theorem with fifteen binders, three of them bundles. At the
eight green rungs five of the fifteen --- six at G1 --- are replaced by checked terms; the rest,
including the whole of \code{hbridge}, stand. The elaboration environment and its model are
universally quantified at every rung, so the ladder gives conditional non-vacuity and not
unconditional truth.
\item Two of the assumed fields are \emph{false in general} and hold only on the measured inputs
(Assumptions~\ref{asm:EraserAsks-kernel_ind_head_true} and~\ref{asm:EraserAsks-block_keys_distinct}).
Every statement taking \code{EraserAsks} is bounded by them.
\item The default \code{\#erase} configuration is \textbf{not} the pinned one: the shipping
\code{ErasureConfig} differs from \code{ConfigPinned} on three of five fields, and a default
invocation is outside every theorem here.
\item The step from the emitted program value to the \code{.ast} bytes peregrine reads is outside
every theorem. The serialiser is a \code{partial def} with no equational lemma, so every
conclusion of this development is a statement about a pair of a $\lbox$ environment and a $\lbox$
term, and never about a byte sequence --- even though it is the byte sequence that
\code{green-check} diffs and that a user actually sees.
\end{itemize}

Where the development departs from [S] and [L], the reasons are recorded at the point of
departure rather than here: applied-form constructors instead of blocks (F-ETA2), no fixpoint
application in the emitted program (F-ETA, which is why expandedness is not concluded),
Lean-specific steps inside $\Lower$, the first-order restriction on the observation, and $\Box$
on the discriminee of a case analysis (F-PROP). What [S] states unconditionally and this
development does not is the environment half of the erasure: there it is part of the certified
pipeline, here it is Assumption~\ref{asm:ErasureBridge-erasesEnv}.
