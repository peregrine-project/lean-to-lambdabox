\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Caveat} The oracle lemmas speak of top-level calls; the induction behind them
  generalizes the binder list.
\item \lead{Caveat} The step lemmas assume \code{NatPlain} and \code{MachineFree}: under
  \code{nat := .machine} they cover only terms without \code{Nat} literals and machine constants,
  as the fragment test does.
\item \lead{Caveat} \code{collectDeps\_ok\_of} is a sufficient condition: the fragment test also
  accepts programs outside its hypotheses.
\end{itemize}
