\section{Program order and scopes}\label{sec:core-order}

A program's model is built one declaration at a time, so a value mentions only its own block and
older declarations. Scopes bound what the traversal may register.

\section{The traversal's steps}\label{sec:core-steps}

\code{erasePure\_erases} is proved by induction on the traversal's fuel. Each case of
\code{visitExpr}, and \code{visitMutual} on a non-recursive declaration, is one step lemma from
the statement at smaller fuels.

\begin{definition}[The statement at one fuel]
  \label{def:EraseProof-ExprSpec}

  \item \code{MutualSpec ... fuel c}: a run of \code{visitMutual} on an unregistered constant of
        \code{S} registers it and \code{Grows}.
  \end{itemize}
  \lead{Reference} \code{erases\_erase} with \code{erase\_global\_erases\_deps}.
\end{definition}

  \item \code{Grows.refl}, \code{Grows.trans}: growth is reflexive and composes.

\section{Recursive blocks}\label{sec:core-blocks}

\code{visitMutual} erases a recursive declaration with its whole block: one fresh fix variable
per member, each value erased, each body closed by \code{mkDef}'s loop, member \code{j} registered
as \code{tFix defs j}.
